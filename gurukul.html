Renting a Flat in Gurukul

Gurukul is a residential and commercial neighbourhood in the Memnagar part of Ahmedabad, built around Gurukul Road. It is a common first choice for tenants who want shops, schools, clinics and public transport within reach.

Akshat Estate has worked in Gurukul and Memnagar for over 20 years, so we can tell you which pockets suit your budget and daily routine, and arrange visits at convenient times.
