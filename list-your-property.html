FAQ

Owners: Common Questions

Quick answers before you list. Need more? Read our rental agreement help.

Does it cost anything to list my property?

Listing your property on Akshat Estate is free. If you have a question about anything else, ask us when we call you.

Which areas do you cover?

We focus on Gurukul, Memnagar and Sola, and we also help owners across Ahmedabad.

What do I need to share to get listed?

The location, property type, BHK, expected rent and furnishing. Then send clear photos of each room on WhatsApp. The form on this page opens WhatsApp with your details filled in so you can attach the photos in the same chat.

Can I list a property for sale as well?

Yes. Choose Sell my property in the form. Flats, offices, shops and other properties are welcome.

Who decides which tenant rents my property?

You do. We help by coordinating visits at convenient times, and the final decision on who rents your property stays with you.

Can you help with the rental agreement?

Yes. We help with the rental agreement and paperwork before move-in. The rental agreement help page explains what a typical agreement covers and what to keep ready.

Can I change the rent or take my listing down later?

Yes. Message or call us and we will update the listing or remove it.

Prefer to talk directly?

Call or message us and we will guide you through listing your property.
