Things to Check Before Renting a Flat in Ahmedabad

A practical checklist for checking a rental property, agreement, costs, amenities and condition before moving in.

Before paying a deposit or signing a rental agreement, inspect the home carefully and clarify the terms with the owner or property representative. A short checklist can help prevent misunderstandings later.

Property checklist

  • Check walls, ceilings, doors, windows and visible plumbing.
  • Test lights, fans, switches, taps and basic fixtures.
  • Confirm water supply, lift, parking and security arrangements.
  • Ask which appliances or furniture are included.
  • Clarify maintenance and utility responsibilities.

Agreement and payment details

Read the rental agreement carefully and confirm rent, deposit, notice period, lock-in terms and other charges. Keep copies of the documents and payment records.

A property visit is the best way to verify details that photographs and listings cannot fully show.
