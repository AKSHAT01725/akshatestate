1 RK vs 1 BHK: Which Should You Rent?

Compare 1 RK and 1 BHK homes by space, privacy, budget and everyday practicality.

The main difference between a 1 RK and a 1 BHK is the separation of the bedroom. In a 1 RK, the room and kitchen form a more compact layout. A 1 BHK has a separate bedroom, which can provide more privacy and flexibility.

When a 1 RK may suit you

  • You want a compact home with a lower overall housing cost.
  • You live alone and do not need a separate bedroom.
  • You prefer a smaller space that is easier to maintain.

When a 1 BHK may suit you

  • You want a separate bedroom for privacy.
  • You need space for a couple, guest or work-from-home setup.
  • You expect to stay for a longer period and want more flexibility.
