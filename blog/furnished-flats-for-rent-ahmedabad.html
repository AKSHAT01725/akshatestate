Furnished Flats for Rent in Ahmedabad

A practical guide to furnished, semi-furnished and unfurnished rental homes in Ahmedabad: what is usually included and what to check before you commit.

Furnished, semi-furnished or unfurnished? The right choice depends on how long you plan to stay, what furniture you already own and your monthly budget. Here is what each option usually includes and what to check before you decide.

What furnished, semi-furnished and unfurnished usually mean

  • Unfurnished: an empty home, normally with basic fixtures such as lights, fans and bathroom fittings.
  • Semi-furnished: typically the basics such as wardrobes and kitchen cabinets or a modular kitchen, and sometimes a few appliances.
  • Furnished: ready to move in, with items such as beds, a sofa, an AC and kitchen appliances.

These terms are not standardised. What counts as “furnished” differs from one owner to another, so always ask for the exact list of items.

Who each option suits

  • Furnished: professionals relocating to Ahmedabad, shorter stays, or anyone who does not want to buy furniture.
  • Semi-furnished: families who want the wardrobes and kitchen set up but prefer to bring their own furniture.
  • Unfurnished: longer stays, or tenants who already own furniture and want to keep monthly rent lower.

What to check before renting a furnished flat

  • Ask for a written list of everything included and keep it with the rental agreement.
  • Test the items on the list: AC, geyser, fridge, washing machine, lights and fans.
  • Check the condition of the mattress, sofa and other soft furnishings.
  • Confirm who repairs or replaces an appliance if it stops working.
  • Ask how damage and normal wear are handled when you move out, and how that affects your deposit.
  • Ask whether maintenance is included in the rent, and remember that AC use will raise your electricity bill.

How furnishing affects the rent

Furnished homes usually cost more per month than a similar unfurnished home because furniture and appliances are provided. Compare that extra rent with the cost and effort of furnishing a home yourself. Our guide on how much it costs to rent a flat in Ahmedabad covers the other costs to plan for.
