How to read the result

  • The 30% guideline. Many people aim to keep rent, maintenance and bills at around 30% of take-home income. It is a rule of thumb, not a rule. Your own commitments and savings goals matter more.
  • Cash to move in. This is usually the surprise. The first month’s rent, the security deposit, any brokerage and the cost of moving all fall due in the same week.
  • Check what the rent includes. Maintenance, parking and water charges differ between buildings. Ask before you decide.
