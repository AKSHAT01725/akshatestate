FAQ

Renting in Ahmedabad: Common Questions

Quick answers for tenants and owners.

How do I find a flat for rent with Akshat Estate?

Use the search at the top of this page or open the Rent page, then message us on WhatsApp from any property card. If you do not see what you need, fill in the Tell Us What You Need form and we will look for matching homes.

Which areas in Ahmedabad do you cover?

We focus on Gurukul, Memnagar and Sola, and can also help with other parts of Ahmedabad. Each area page lists nearby landmarks and the rent range of the flats we currently have.

Can I see the flat before I decide?

Yes. We help coordinate property visits at times that suit you. Message us on WhatsApp with the property you like and a convenient time.

Can bachelors rent a flat?

It depends on the owner and the housing society. On the Rent page you can switch on the “Bachelors allowed” filter, or tell us in the requirement form and we will check with owners.

What documents do I need to rent a flat in Ahmedabad?

Requirements vary by owner and society. Read our guide to the documents required for renting a flat in Ahmedabad and we will confirm what is needed for the home you choose.

How much does it cost to rent a flat in Ahmedabad?

Rent depends on the area, size, furnishing and building, and there are other costs such as a security deposit and maintenance. Our guide explains how much it costs to rent a flat in Ahmedabad, and each area page shows the rent range of flats currently listed with us.

What is the difference between furnished, semi-furnished and unfurnished?

Furnished homes are usually ready to move in with items such as beds, a sofa and appliances. Semi-furnished homes typically have basics such as wardrobes and a kitchen setup. Unfurnished homes are empty apart from basic fixtures. Always ask for the exact list of items. More in our guide to furnished flats for rent in Ahmedabad.

I own a flat. Can you help me find a tenant?

Yes. Share the details on the List Your Property page and we will get in touch to discuss the next steps.
