FAQ

Renting a Flat: Common Questions

Quick answers before you shortlist.

How do I narrow down the flats?

Use the filters above the list: locality, BHK, furnishing, budget, and the quick options for bachelors allowed, parking and furnished homes. You can also sort by newest, price or area. Tap the heart on any flat to save it, then compare your saved homes on the My Shortlist page.

What is the difference between furnished, semi-furnished and unfurnished?

Furnished homes are usually ready to move in, semi-furnished homes typically have wardrobes and a kitchen setup, and unfurnished homes are empty apart from basic fixtures. The exact items differ from owner to owner, so ask for the list. See our guide to furnished flats for rent in Ahmedabad.

Can bachelors rent a flat?

It depends on the owner and the housing society. Switch on the “Bachelors allowed” filter, or tell us in the form below and we will check with owners.

Can I see the flat before I decide?

Yes. We help coordinate property visits at times that suit you. Message us on WhatsApp with the property you like and a convenient time.

How much will I pay in total?

Besides the monthly rent, plan for a security deposit, maintenance if it is charged separately, and the cost of the rental agreement. Our guide explains how much it costs to rent a flat in Ahmedabad.

What if I do not see what I am looking for?

Fill in the Tell Us What You Need form below with your area, budget and BHK. We will look for matching homes and get back to you.
