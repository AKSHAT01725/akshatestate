Privacy Policy

We value your privacy and aim to handle your information responsibly when you browse our website or contact us about a property.

Last updated: October 2026

1. Information We Collect

When you submit an enquiry, contact us, request a property viewing, tell us your requirement, list a property, or otherwise communicate with Akshat Estate, we may receive information such as your name, phone number, email address, property preferences (area, size, budget, move-in date) and anything else you choose to write.

If you start an enquiry on WhatsApp from a property or a form on this website, we may also keep a record of that enquiry, such as which property you asked about, even if you do not send the WhatsApp message.

We also receive basic usage information through website analytics, such as the pages viewed and the type of device and browser used.

2. How We Use Information

We may use the information you provide to respond to enquiries, discuss available properties, arrange communication or viewings, provide real-estate assistance, and improve our website and services.

3. Information Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. Your enquiry details are stored with Google Firebase, the service that runs our enquiry records and admin panel, and are sent to us by email through EmailJS. These providers handle the data on our behalf to run the website.

Information may also be shared when reasonably necessary to respond to your enquiry (for example, with a property owner so that a visit can be arranged), to provide a service you requested, or to comply with applicable law.

4. Cookies, Browser Storage & Analytics

The website uses Google Analytics (through Firebase) to understand which pages are used. This can set cookies or similar identifiers in your browser.

The site also saves a few things in your own browser so it works the way you expect: your shortlist of saved homes, your last search filters, a short-lived copy of the property list so pages load faster, and whether you have seen the website notice. If you install the website as an app on your phone, the browser also keeps copies of pages, fonts and images for faster loading and for an offline message. This information stays on your device.

Property pages show a Google Map of the area, which loads content from Google. You can manage or clear cookies and site data in your browser settings.

5. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information submitted through the website. However, no internet transmission or electronic storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

6. Third-Party Links and Services

Our website includes links and contact options for third-party services such as WhatsApp, Google Maps and Instagram. Their privacy practices are governed by their own policies, which we recommend reviewing separately.

7. Your Choices

You may choose what information you provide through our enquiry forms. If you have a question about information you have submitted to us or would like to request an update, please contact us using the details below.

8. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or how your information is handled, contact Akshat Estate at akshatestate.ahd@gmail.com or call +91 81412 93057.
